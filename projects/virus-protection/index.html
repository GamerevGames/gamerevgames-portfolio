Overview

Virus Protection is a first-person shooter set inside of the human body, where the player fights hostile viruses as part of the immune system. It was built in Unreal Engine 4.27 with blueprints and C++ by a five-person team for a university game development course.

I was responsible for core gameplay programming. I also took part in QA and playtesting, and helped teammates with lighting and level design.

  • Three enemy virus types, each with its own AI behavior.
  • Projectile mechanics, including spawn position and damage.
  • A complete, playable level with interactable objects.

About Me

Hi, I'm Travis Carpenter, an independent game developer and the person behind Gamerev Games. I've been working with Unity and C# for over six years, and I've been making games since I was a teenager.

I make games because I love seeing people enjoy them. I specialize in third-person action with a driving narrative, and I'm currently deep in the development of Krimson Kore, my long-term project, alongside a few smaller games on the side.

I share my progress on YouTube through development updates, mechanics breakdowns, and tutorials aimed at helping other developers. I'm open to collaboration of all kinds, so if you have a project, an idea, or just want to talk games, get in touch!

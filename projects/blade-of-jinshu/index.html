Overview

Blade of Jinshu is a third-person action game built around fast, stylish melee combat in the vein of Devil May Cry, with 3D puzzles and combat challenges. It's my first commercial release on Steam.

The project was built with combat at the forefront, so I focused on a modular, data driven attack system, responsive feel, and the feedback effects that make every hit feel worth it.

  • Modular Combos: every move is a ScriptableObject that defines its animation, timing, damage, force, and more, so new attacks can be added without touching code.
  • Finisher Moves: special attacks pause input and reposition the player and target before playing out.
  • Smooth Attack Movement: an interpolation system glides the player to an attack offset, ensuring the hit will land.
  • Combat Feedback: procedural blood, shockwaves, weapon trails, and a character lightning trail effect.

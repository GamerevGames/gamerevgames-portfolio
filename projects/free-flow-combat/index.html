Overview

Free Flow Combat is a third-person combat system built in Unity, inspired by the free flow combat system of the Batman: Arkham series. It lets players chain attacks fluidly across a group of enemies, with movement and combos that flow naturally from one hit to the next.

I built this project at University, starting with a proof-of-concept prototype, and developing it into a polished combat experience with impact effects, reactive enemies, and a small arena setting.

  • Smooth Attack Movement: the player glides to an attack position calculated from the camera angle and input direction.
  • Dynamic Targeting: attacks find and chain between enemies in the fight.
  • Impactful Feedback: shockwaves, particles, sound effects, and subtle camera shake on every hit.
  • Reactive Enemies: the attack type is passed to the enemy so it plays a matching reaction animation.
  • Clean and Modular Code: easy to understand and implement in existing projects.
